# 
### 安装
1. 登陆[Ecwid店铺后台](https://my.ecwid.com/cp)，在Ecwid APP应用市场找到Oceanpayment安装：[https://www.ecwid.com/apps/featured/oceanpayment](https://www.ecwid.com/apps/featured/oceanpayment)
<img src={require('/img/cn/saas/install/ecwid.png').default} className="image-card"/>
<img src={require('/img/cn/saas/install/ecwid1.png').default} className="image-card"/>

### 配置信息
1. 填写Oceanpayment提供的配置信息，**Terminal**、**SecureCode**，点击返回系统自动保存。
<img src={require('/img/cn/saas/install/ecwid2.png').default} className="image-card"/>

### 卸载
1. 在【**Payments**】- 找到Oceanpayment支付方式，点击**Remove**。
<img src={require('/img/cn/saas/install/ecwid3.png').default} className="image-card"/>
